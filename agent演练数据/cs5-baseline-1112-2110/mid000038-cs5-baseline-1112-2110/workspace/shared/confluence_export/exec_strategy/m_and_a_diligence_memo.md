## Board Status

**BOARD_APPROVAL_PENDING**: The Board of Directors has reviewed the preliminary term sheet at the February 15, 2025 meeting. Formal approval is pending completion of:
1. Full financial audit of Nova Analytics FY2024 statements

Board vote is tentatively scheduled for the March 20, 2025 meeting.

## Key Risk Factors

1. **Customer Concentration**: Top 3 customers (Acme Corp, Globex Industries, Initech) represent 45% of Nova's ARR. Loss of any one would materially impact the acquisition thesis.
2. **Technology Integration**: Nova's platform uses a different tech stack (Go/React) versus our Python/Vue stack. Integration timeline estimated at 18-24 months.
3. **Team Retention**: Nova's CTO and 3 of 5 principal engineers have change-of-control provisions requiring retention packages totaling approximately $12M.
4. **Regulatory**: Preliminary FTC/DOJ analysis suggests no antitrust concerns given the complementary nature of the products.
