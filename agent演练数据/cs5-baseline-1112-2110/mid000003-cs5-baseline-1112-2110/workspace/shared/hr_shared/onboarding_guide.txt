Employee Onboarding Guide — 2026 Edition

Welcome to the team! This guide covers everything you need to know about your first month.

1. Pre-boarding (before day 1)
   - IT will ship your laptop and accessories
   - HR will send your offer letter and benefits package
   - Your manager will reach out with a第一天 schedule

2. Week 1
   - Attend new hire orientation (Monday 9am, Conf Room A)
   - Complete I-9 verification with HR
   - Set up your 401(k) through the benefits portal
   - Enroll in health insurance through Workday

3. Week 2
   - Complete product training (self-paced modules)
   - Meet your mentor/buddy
   - Join team standups

4. Company Resources
   - Internal Wiki: wiki.company.internal
   - HR Portal: hr.company.internal
   - IT Helpdesk: helpdesk.company.internal
   - Shared Drive: //company-files/shared/

5. Key Contacts

   - IT Support: it-help@company.com
