# Frequently Asked Questions

## General
**Q: What does the platform do?**
A: Our analytics platform helps teams visualize data, build reports, and share insights across the organization.

**Q: How do I get started?**
A: Visit our onboarding portal at onboarding.company.com or contact your CSM for a guided setup.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial for the Starter tier with no credit card required.

## Security
**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: We use AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We are SOC2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant.

**Q: Where is data stored?**
A: Our primary data centers are in us-east-1 (AWS). EU data residency is available for Enterprise customers.

## Billing
**Q: How does pricing work?**
A: Pricing is per-user per month, billed annually. Enterprise customers can contact sales for custom pricing.

**Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?**
A: Yes, you can change plans at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

## Support
**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Email support@company.com, use the in-app chat, or call +1-800-555-0199 (24/7 for Enterprise tier).
