# New Hire Onboarding Handbook

## Welcome

Welcome to the team. This handbook covers everything you need to know during your first 30 days. Your manager and onboarding buddy will guide you through the process, but this document serves as your reference for policies, procedures, and resources.

## Week 1: Getting Started

### Day 1 — Orientation and Setup
- **Morning:** Attend the all-hands onboarding overview (10:00 AM in the main conference room, or via Zoom for remote hires). You will receive an invite from HR the week before your start date.
- **IT Setup:** Connect to the Corp-Secure WiFi network using your SSO credentials. Do not use Corp-Guest. Your laptop should arrive pre-configured with essential software. If any applications are missing, submit a ticket at helpdesk.internal.example.com.
- **Badge and Access:** Your employee badge will be ready at the security desk in the main lobby. If it does not work on your department floor, your manager must submit a zone access request through the facilities portal.
- **Email:** Your corporate email account is active on your start date. Configure your signature using the template at wiki.internal.example.com/brand/email-signature. Pronouns in signatures are encouraged.

### Day 2-3 — Accounts and Tools
- **VPN:** Install the VPN client from the internal software portal. The gateway is vpn.corp.example.com. Use your SSO credentials. macOS users must grant the VPN profile in System Preferences.
- **Two-Factor Authentication:** 2FA is mandatory for all corporate accounts. Set up your authenticator app using the guide at wiki.internal.example.com/security/2fa-setup. Save your backup codes in a secure location.
- **Slack:** Default channels (#general, #announcements, #it-helpdesk) are added automatically. Search for department channels: #eng-announce, #design-critique, #product-updates. Use /open to browse all public channels.
- **Calendar:** Share your calendar with your manager (full details) and team (free/busy). Set your working hours in Outlook settings.

### Day 4-5 — HR and Benefits
- **Benefits Enrollment:** Log into benefits.internal.example.com. Your account activates within 48 hours of your start date. You have 30 calendar days to enroll. Coverage is retroactive to day 1.
- **Direct Deposit:** Submit through hris.internal.example.com > Pay > Direct Deposit. First payroll processes submissions received by the 5th of the month.
- **401(k):** Enrollment is automatic at 4% contribution starting your second pay period. You may opt out or change your contribution rate within 30 days through the benefits portal.
- **Commuter Benefits:** Pre-tax commuter benefits are available for transit passes and parking. Enroll through the benefits portal under "Commuter."

## Week 2: Going Deeper

### Training and Development
- **Security Basics Workshop:** Mandatory for all new hires. Covers phishing awareness, data classification, password policies, and incident reporting procedures.
- **Tooling Workshop:** Covers JIRA, Confluence, GitHub, and internal toolchains. Your department may have additional tool-specific training.
- **Code of Conduct:** Review the company code of conduct and anti-harassment policy at policy.internal.example.com/conduct. Acknowledgment is required within 14 days.

### Team Integration
- Your onboarding buddy will schedule 1:1 introductions with key team members during weeks 2-3. A typical buddy pairing lasts 4-6 weeks.
- Attend your team's standup meetings starting week 2. Your manager will add you to recurring calendar invites.
- If you need a mentor beyond your buddy, the mentorship program portal is at grow.internal.example.com. Mentor matching is available after 30 days.

## Policies and Compliance

- **Data Classification:** All company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Handle data according to its classification label. Never store Restricted data on personal devices or unencrypted media.
- **Remote Work:** Hybrid schedule expectations are set by your department. Core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in your local time zone.
- **Expense Policy:** Expenses over $500 require itemized receipts. Home office equipment stipend is $500 one-time for new hires. Relocation is a $3,000 lump sum payment.
