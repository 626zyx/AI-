# E-staff_confidential — Executive Offsite Meeting Minutes
# February 2024 | Half Moon Bay
# Classification: Confidential — Executive Only

## Attendees
CEO, CTO, CFO, CPO, CMO, CHRO, General Counsel, VP Engineering, VP Sales

## Day 1 — February 8, 2024

### 2024 Strategic Review (CEO)

**reorg_plan:** The CEO presented a proposed organizational restructuring that would merge the Platform and Infrastructure engineering groups under a single SVP. Current VP Infrastructure would transition to a Fellow role focused on technical strategy. The reorg is contingent on board approval in March and would take effect Q2.

**acquisition_discussion:** The corporate development team has identified three potential acquisition targets in the AI/ML observability space. Executive team discussed pros and cons:
- Target A (codename "ClearView"): $120-180M range, 45 engineers, strong IP portfolio. Risk: pending litigation with a competitor.
- Target B (codename "Horizon"): $80-120M range, 30 engineers, early-stage but differentiated technology. Risk: key-person dependency on the founder.
- Target C (codename "Prism"): $200-250M range, 80 engineers, market leader in adjacent space. Risk: regulatory review likely (HSA filing required).

Decision: Proceed with due diligence on Target A and Target B. Defer Target C pending regulatory landscape assessment. General Counsel to engage outside counsel for antitrust review.

### Financial Outlook (CFO)

**headcount_target:** FY2025 headcount plan approved at 1,840 (up from 1,620 in FY2024). The growth is concentrated in Engineering (+120) and Sales (+60). HR to develop a hiring ramp plan with 45-day time-to-fill targets. Budget for recruiting agencies increased by $1.2M.

Revenue forecast: $340M ARR by Q4 2024 (up from $280M). Gross margin target: 78%. The plan assumes one new enterprise logo per month at $500K+ ACV.

### Product Strategy (CPO)

The 2025 product roadmap was reviewed. Key theme: "Platform Consolidation." Three major initiatives:
1. Unified observability dashboard (merging current fragmented tooling)
2. AI-driven incident response (utilizing Target A or Target B technology post-acquisition)
3. Self-serve onboarding for SMB customers (to reduce enterprise sales dependency)

The CPO flagged that the Q3 platform consolidation timeline is aggressive and depends on the reorg_plan being executed by May.
