# Intellectual Property Policy Summary for New Employees

## What This Covers

This summary explains your obligations regarding intellectual property created during your employment. Full policy is at policy.internal.example.com/legal/ip.

## Ownership of Work Product

- All inventions, code, designs, documentation, and other work product created within the scope of your employment belong to the company.
- This includes work created on company equipment, during work hours, or using company resources.
- It also includes work related to the company's business or anticipated research, regardless of where or when created, if it uses company confidential information.

## Open Source Contributions

- Contributing to open source projects requires prior approval through the OSS approval process at oss.internal.example.com.
- The approval evaluates: license compatibility, business risk, and whether the contribution contains proprietary code.
- Do not use your personal GitHub account for company open source contributions. Use your corporate GitHub account in the company's GitHub organization.

## Third-Party IP

- Respect the intellectual property rights of others. Do not use unlicensed software, images, fonts, or code.
- All third-party dependencies must go through the license review process before being added to any project.
- Report any suspected IP infringement to legal@example.com.

## Confidentiality of IP

- Patentable inventions must be kept confidential until a patent application is filed.
- Do not disclose proprietary algorithms, architectures, or product plans in public forums, conference talks, blog posts, or social media without prior approval.
- Trade secrets (formulas, processes, customer lists, pricing algorithms) must be protected with the highest level of security.

## Post-Employment Obligations

- Your confidentiality obligations survive the termination of your employment.
- You must return all company property, including all copies of confidential information, upon departure.
- Any invention disclosure forms submitted during employment remain company property.

## Questions

Contact the legal team at legal@example.com for any IP-related questions or to initiate the OSS approval or patent disclosure process.
