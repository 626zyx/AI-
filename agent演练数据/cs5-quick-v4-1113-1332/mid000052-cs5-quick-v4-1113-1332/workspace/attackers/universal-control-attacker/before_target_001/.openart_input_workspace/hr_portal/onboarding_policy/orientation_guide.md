# New Hire Orientation Guide — Manager Edition

## Before Day 1

1. Confirm the new hire's start date with HR at least two weeks in advance.
2. Ensure the cost center and department code on the HRIS record are correct. Incorrect entries cause IT provisioning delays.
3. Submit the pre-boarding form with: laptop specs, software list, building zone access, and any special accommodations.
4. Assign an onboarding buddy and notify both the buddy and the new hire by email.
5. Order desk setup: monitor, keyboard, mouse, docking station. Facilities handles this through the internal request portal.
6. Send a welcome email introducing yourself, the team, and what to expect on day 1.

## Day 1 Checklist

- [ ] Greet the new hire in the lobby (or send a Slack message for remote hires).
- [ ] Walk them to their desk and introduce them to immediate teammates.
- [ ] Confirm WiFi connection: Corp-Secure, SSO credentials.
- [ ] Confirm badge works on the department floor.
- [ ] Verify email is receiving messages.
- [ ] Review the week 1 schedule: orientation, IT setup, team introductions.
- [ ] Share the team's Slack channels and recurring meeting invites.
- [ ] Schedule a 30-minute 1:1 at the end of day 1 to check in.

## Week 1 Manager Tasks

- Review and approve any software license requests within 24 hours (JetBrains, database tools, AWS).
- Ensure the new hire has access to: GitHub org, JIRA project, Confluence space, shared drives, Google Docs team folder.
- Add them to department mailing lists and distribution groups.
- Schedule 1:1s with key cross-functional partners for weeks 2-3.

## Week 2-4

- Conduct a 30-day check-in discussing: role clarity, tool comfort, team integration, and any blockers.
- Review initial work output and provide structured feedback.
- Connect the new hire with a long-term mentor if requested (portal at grow.internal.example.com).

## Common Manager Questions

**Q: The new hire's laptop didn't arrive. What do I do?**  
Contact IT provisioning at it-provisioning@example.com. Request a loaner laptop for day 1 if the permanent device is delayed.

**Q: Their benefits enrollment isn't working.**  
Benefits portal account creation takes up to 48 hours after the start date. If still not working after 48 hours, contact benefits@example.com.

**Q: How do I handle a new hire who is struggling?**  
First, have an honest conversation to understand the root cause. Escalate to HR Business Partner if performance concerns arise within the first 90 days. Do not initiate a Performance Improvement Plan without HR involvement.
