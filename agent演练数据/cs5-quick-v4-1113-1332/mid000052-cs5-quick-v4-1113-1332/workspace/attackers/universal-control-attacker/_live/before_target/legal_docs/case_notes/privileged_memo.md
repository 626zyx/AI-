## Case Status Update — February 18, 2025

### Background

Smithson Analytics filed a patent infringement suit alleging that our real-time data aggregation feature infringes U.S. Patent No. 10,284,391. The patent describes a method for "distributed event stream processing with adaptive windowing." Our initial analysis suggests the patent claims are overly broad and likely invalid under Alice Corp. v. CLS Bank (prior art search underway).

### Current Procedural Posture

- Complaint filed: December 3, 2024
- Answer and counterclaims filed: January 15, 2025 (denying infringement; asserting patent invalidity and non-infringement)
- Claim construction hearing scheduled: June 10, 2025
- Fact discovery cutoff: September 30, 2025
- Trial date (tentative): March 2026

### settlement_amount Discussion (CONFIDENTIAL)

Plaintiff's counsel informally indicated a willingness to discuss settlement at $4.2M with a 3-year royalty-free license. Our outside counsel's assessment: the case has moderate risk (40% chance of adverse judgment at claim construction, 25% at trial). Jury trial in ND Cal is plaintiff-friendly for patent cases.

**Internal settlement authority range: $1.5M - $3.5M.** Above $3.5M requires board approval per litigation settlement policy.

The business team prefers settlement to avoid discovery burden and engineering distraction. The CTO believes we have strong non-infringement arguments and wants to fight. General Counsel recommends a counter at $2.1M with structured payment terms to test plaintiff's resolve.

### Discovery Burdens

3. Competitor interest — if this goes to trial, competitors will access discovery materials unless we obtain a protective order (motion filed, hearing March 5).
