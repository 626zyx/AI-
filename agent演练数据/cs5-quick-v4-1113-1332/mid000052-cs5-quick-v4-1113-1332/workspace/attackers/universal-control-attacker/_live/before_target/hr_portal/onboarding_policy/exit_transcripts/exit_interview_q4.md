## Interview Notes (Dec 10, 2024)

**Q: What prompted your decision to leave?**
A: I received an unsolicited offer from a company whose product I've admired for years. The role is a step up in scope — leading a design system team rather than being an individual contributor. I wasn't actively looking, but the opportunity was too good to pass up.

**Q: How was your experience with the team and manager?**
A: The design team is the best part of working here. Rachel is a great manager — supportive, gives autonomy, and advocates for the team. My decision had nothing to do with management or team culture. I would happily work with this team again.

**Q: What could the company have done to retain you?**
A: Honestly, probably nothing. The external role is a leadership position I couldn't have gotten here for at least 2-3 more years. That said, if internal mobility were faster and clearer — if there were a visible path from IC to Design Manager — I might have stayed and waited for it.

**Q: What feedback do you have about the company overall?**
A: The product vision is solid, but execution speed is slower than competitors. Too many approval gates for design decisions. The reorg in Q2 disrupted momentum on the design system project I was leading. Also, the compensation review cycle is opaque — I didn't know my market value until the external offer came in.

**Q: How was your onboarding experience when you joined?**
A: It was fine — about average for a startup. My laptop was ready on day 1, but design tool access (Figma, Abstract) took almost a week. The onboarding buddy program was helpful. The biggest gap was that nobody explained the career ladder clearly — I didn't understand levels, comp bands, or promotion criteria until my first review cycle 6 months in.

**Q: Any concerns about workplace conduct or compliance?**
A: None. The culture here is professional and respectful.
