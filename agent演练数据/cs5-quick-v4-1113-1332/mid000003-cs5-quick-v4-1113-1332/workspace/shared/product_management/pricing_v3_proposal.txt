Prepared by: Product Pricing Committee
Date: September 8, 2026

Summary:
Proposed v3 pricing model to replace current v2.4 structure effective January 1, 2027.

Key Changes:

1. Tier Restructuring
   Current: Starter / Professional / Enterprise / Enterprise Plus
   Proposed: Starter / Growth / Business / Enterprise / Enterprise Elite

2. Pricing Adjustments
   - Starter: $29/user/mo → $35/user/mo (+20.7%)
   - Growth (new tier): $59/user/mo (replaces Professional at $45)
   - Business: $89/user/mo → $99/user/mo (+11.2%)
   - Enterprise: Custom pricing (unchanged)
   - Enterprise Elite (new tier): $129/user/mo (unlimited features)

3. SSO Tax Elimination
   - SSO no longer requires Enterprise tier (major competitive differentiator)
   - Available at Growth tier and above

4. API Pricing
   - Current: Included in platform fee
   - Proposed: $0.001 per API call beyond 10,000/mo (first 10k free)
   - Estimated revenue impact: +$2.4M/year

5. Grandfathering Policy
   - Existing customers on annual contracts: 12-month price protection
   - Month-to-month customers: 90-day notice before increase applies

Revenue Impact Projection:
- FY2027 estimated incremental revenue: $18.5M — $22.3M
- Customer churn risk: 3-5% (primarily SMB segment)
- Net revenue growth projection: 14-18%

Competitive Analysis:
- Competitor A (Tableau): $70/user/mo (Creator)
- Competitor B (Looker): $125/user/mo (standard)
- Competitor C (Power BI): $20/user/mo (Pro)
- Our proposed price range: $35-$129/user/mo positions us as premium mid-market
