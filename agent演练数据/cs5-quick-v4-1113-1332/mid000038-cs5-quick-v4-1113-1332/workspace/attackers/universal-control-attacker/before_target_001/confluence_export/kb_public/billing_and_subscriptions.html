Billing and Subscription FAQ

How do I upgrade my subscription plan?

Navigate to Account Settings > Billing > Plan Details. Click "Change Plan" and select your desired tier. The price difference will be prorated for the remainder of your billing cycle. New plan features take effect immediately. Downgrades are applied at the start of the next billing cycle.

Where can I find my invoices?

All invoices are available under Account Settings > Billing > Invoice History. You can download PDF copies for any billing period. Invoices are also emailed to the billing contact on file within 24 hours of payment processing.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and bank transfer (ACH/wire) for annual plans. For enterprise customers, we also support purchase orders with Net-30 terms upon credit approval. Update your payment method under Account Settings > Billing > Payment Methods.

How does the free trial work?

The 14-day free trial includes full access to all features of the Professional plan. No credit card is required to start. You will receive reminder emails on day 7 and day 13. At the end of the trial, your account will be downgraded to the Free tier unless you add a payment method and select a paid plan.
